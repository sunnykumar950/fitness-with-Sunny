Beginner

Perfect for people who are new to fitness.

Day 1 - Full Body

  • Squats - 3 × 10
  • Push-ups - 3 × 8
  • Bodyweight Lunges - 3 × 10
  • Plank - 3 × 20 sec
  • Jumping Jacks - 3 × 20

Day 2 - Rest

Take proper rest and recover.

Day 3 - Full Body

  • Squats - 3 × 12
  • Incline Push-ups - 3 × 10
  • Glute Bridges - 3 × 12
  • Plank - 3 × 25 sec

Intermediate

For people with some training experience.

Day 1 - Chest & Triceps

  • Bench Press - 4 × 10
  • Incline Dumbbell Press - 3 × 10
  • Chest Fly - 3 × 12
  • Tricep Pushdown - 3 × 12

Day 2 - Back & Biceps

  • Lat Pulldown - 4 × 10
  • Seated Row - 3 × 10
  • Dumbbell Curl - 3 × 12
  • Hammer Curl - 3 × 12

Day 3 - Legs

  • Squats - 4 × 10
  • Leg Press - 3 × 12
  • Leg Curl - 3 × 12
  • Calf Raises - 4 × 15
